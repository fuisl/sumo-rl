## The core aesthetic: "hand-drawn warmth meets minimal geometry"

Anthropic's illustration style sits between two things that don't usually go together: loose, imperfect hand-drawing and tight, minimal geometric flat-color icons. Your reference grid shows both modes coexisting.

### 1. Two distinct illustration modes (and when each is used)

**Mode A — Line-art sketches (black ink, single weight)**
- Uneven, hand-drawn contour lines — sprouts, hands, stacked stones, a hand with a bee/insect
- Lines have a slightly wobbly, organic imperfection — never ruler-straight, never a perfect Bézier curve
- Minimal to zero interior detail — shapes are defined almost entirely by outline
- Used for: growth, care, craft, human touch, nuance/complexity concepts

**Mode B — Flat silhouette icons (single flat color background, white/cream cutout shape)**
- Solid color card background (orange, green, terracotta)
- One simple white silhouette centered — a building/institution, a head in profile with a sparkle inside, a branching tree/network
- No gradients, no shading, no outlines on the silhouette itself
- Used for: institutions, systems, abstract concepts (thought, knowledge, structure)

Nothing is saturated or neon. Every color reads as "warm, dusty, slightly desaturated" — closer to natural pigments (terracotta clay, sage leaf, oat paper) than digital brights.

### 3. Composition: modular grid of "cards"
- The whole reference is a masonry/bento grid of square-ish and rectangular tiles, each with its own flat background color
- Each tile = one self-contained visual idea, no scene bleeds across tiles
- Generous internal padding — the illustration sits centered with breathing room, never touching the tile edges
- Tile backgrounds rotate through: cream, light gray, terracotta, sage green, dusty blue, tan/camel

### 4. Line & shape rules
- **Stroke weight**: consistent, moderate thickness (not hairline, not bold marker) throughout a single illustration
- **Imperfection is intentional**: circles aren't perfectly round, lines have slight variance — it should look drawn by hand in one confident pass, not vector-perfect
- **No gradients, no drop shadows, no 3D** anywhere in either mode
- **Rounded, soft forms**: stacked stones, rounded heads, blobby leaves — very few sharp corners except in the architectural/building icon
- **Metaphor-first subjects**: hands (care, craft, giving), plants/sprouts (growth), stacked stones (balance/trust), a head with a radiating spark (thought/insight), a branching tree (network/knowledge), a building with columns (institution/governance), paper + insect (attention to detail, small things)

- Body: **Lora**
- Fallbacks: Arial / Georgia
